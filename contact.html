Good to know

Questions,answered.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and prom season (April–June), we recommend booking 4–8 weeks ahead to secure your preferred vehicle. For nights out and transfers, a few days is usually fine, and we'll always try to accommodate last-minute requests.

How is pricing calculated?

Most bookings are priced by the hour, based on the vehicle, date and itinerary. Point-to-point transfers can be quoted as a flat rate. Send us your plans and we'll provide a clear, competitive quote with no hidden extras.

Do you decorate cars for weddings?

Yes. Ribbons and classic wedding décor are available for our limousines and the Rolls-Royce. Let us know your colours and we'll prepare the car to match.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Surrey and serve all of Metro Vancouver and the Lower Mainland, including Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, Delta, Langley, Abbotsford and beyond. Ask us about longer trips.

Can we make multiple stops?

Absolutely. Photo locations, venue changes and pickups at several homes are all part of the service. Just include them in your request so we can plan the timing.
